How It Works

From sign-up to your interview in four simple steps

  1. 1

    Sign up

    Fill out a short form with your email and the times that work for you.

  2. 2

    Get matched

    We pair you with 1 or 2 high school tutors who fit your schedule.

  3. 3

    Meet on Zoom

    Join a friendly 30-minute Zoom session each week.

  4. 4

    Pass the test

    Walk into your USCIS interview ready and confident.

When we meet

Sessions are 30 minutes on Zoom. You choose the times that work for you (Pacific Time):

  • Monday – Friday4:00 PM – 10:00 PM
  • Saturday – Sunday9:00 AM – 10:00 PM

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. ICSC tutoring is completely free.

Who are the tutors?

Trained Interlake High School students. You'll be matched with 1 or 2 tutors.

How do sessions work?

You meet your tutor on Zoom for 30 minutes each week, at a time you choose.

What do I need to sign up?

Only an email address. Everything else on the form is optional.

Where can I practice on my own?

On CivicPreps.com — free practice for all 128 civics questions, plus reading, writing and speaking.
