About the Club

A student-run program at Interlake High School

Interlake Citizenship Services Club (ICSC) is a student-run program that pairs aspiring U.S. citizens with trained high school tutors. We provide friendly, patient support in the English language skills essential for passing the citizenship interview — completely free of charge.

Who we are

Our tutors are trained Interlake High School students. Each student is matched with 1 or 2 tutors, who meet with them on Zoom every week until their interview.

1–2tutors per student
30minutes per weekly session
100%free — no cost, ever

What we cover

Your tutors prepare you for all four parts of the naturalization test.

Civics

Practice the official USCIS questions on U.S. history and government.

Reading

Read sentences with the official USCIS vocabulary, clearly and confidently.

Writing

Master sentence dictation from the USCIS writing word list.

Speaking

Get comfortable answering interview questions out loud with a real person.
